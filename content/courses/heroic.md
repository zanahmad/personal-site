**Advanced Topics in Computational Cardiology: 3D Heart Modeling, Shape Analysis and AI**<br>
Johns Hopkins University · HEROIC (EN.500.312) · Fall 2024<br>
Course creator and instructor: Zan Ahmad

This course introduced cardiovascular modeling, numerical simulation, anatomical shape analysis, and machine learning through research questions and coding workshops. The selected samples below have been edited for standalone reading while retaining their original teaching purpose.

<h2>Feedback control in a circulation model</h2>

A project brief that moves from a fixed heart rate to pressure feedback and a time-varying cardiac clock. It asks students to design experiments, compare model responses, and check their numerical implementation. Intended for readers familiar with ordinary differential equations; an existing pulsatile circulation model is required.

[Read the project](/teaching/samples/heroic-circulation-control/) · [PDF (3 pages)](/files/teaching-samples/heroic-circulation-control.pdf)

<h2>Transport and diffusion with finite differences</h2>

A self-contained Python tutorial on translating differential equations into array updates. Linear transport and physical diffusion provide a concrete way to explore boundary conditions, stability, and numerical error. Adapted from the course’s asynchronous tutorial, with credit to Barba and Forsyth’s CFD Python materials.

[Read the tutorial](/teaching/samples/heroic-finite-differences/) · [PDF (3 pages)](/files/teaching-samples/heroic-finite-differences.pdf) · [Python code](/files/teaching-samples/heroic-finite-differences.py)
