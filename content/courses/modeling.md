**Mathematical Modeling and Computer Simulations in Physiology and Medicine**

Course creator and instructor: Zan Ahmad.

- [Fall 2024 syllabus (PDF)](/files/Fall_2024_HEART_Syllabus.pdf)

The course uses Frank C. Hoppensteadt and Charles S. Peskin's *Modeling and Simulation in Medicine and the Life Sciences* as a mathematical background. Guided calculations and code accompany the lecture notes.
