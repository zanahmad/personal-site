\section*{Conservation of volume --- review sheet}
Use this after attempting the worked examples and practice. Explain each claim in words before calculating.

\section*{Key ideas}
\begin{itemize}
\item Choose a question and boundary before calculating.
\item A stock has amount units; a rate has amount/time units.
\item Final storage =\allowbreak{} initial storage +\allowbreak{} total entry - total exit.
\item Equal flows maintain storage even when fluid moves.
\item An internal transfer cancels when the boundary includes both regions.
\item Numerical checks and measurements test different claims.
\end{itemize}
\section*{Common errors and repairs}
\textbf{Adding a rate directly to a volume.} Multiply the rate by a compatible elapsed time first.

\textbf{Counting internal flow as a loss from the combined system.} Cancel the matched departure and arrival.

\textbf{Accepting a negative stored volume because the arithmetic ran.} Locate the emptying event and revise the outflow assumption.

\section*{Retrieve without your notes}
\begin{itemize}
\item What units distinguish volume from flow?
\item Can 100 mL/min enter a steady-volume reservoir?
\item What independent evidence would challenge a correct budget program?
\end{itemize}

\end{itemize}
