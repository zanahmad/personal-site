\section*{Shape comparisons, deformation penalties and geometric matching --- review sheet}
Use this after attempting the worked examples and practice. Explain each claim in words before calculating.

\section*{Key ideas}
\begin{itemize}
\item A distance requires a representation, units, correspondence and declared invariances.
\item Finite cyclic shifts remove the choice of first index while preserving direction and local order.
\item Proper rigid fits preserve physical size.
\item The finite score separates amplitude and neighbor variation on a fixed grid.
\item Elastic path energy and endpoint matching have distinct roles.
\item Synthetic grouping is evidence about the implemented comparison, not clinical categories.
\end{itemize}
\section*{Common errors and repairs}
\textbf{Comparing shades across matrices with different color scales.} Read numerical entries and each colorbar in mm.

\textbf{Calling a quadratic score a full elastic geodesic.} Document the actual fixed-array calculation and its missing path/geometry operations.

\textbf{Assuming a tiny endpoint error implies a cheap deformation.} Distinguish endpoint fidelity from path cost.

\section*{Retrieve without your notes}
\begin{itemize}
\item Which known pairs should have near-zero rigid/cyclic RMS?
\item How does E change when every displacement is doubled?
\item What happens to the roughness term for a constant displacement?
\end{itemize}
\section*{Readiness check}
\begin{itemize}

\item Write a small synthetic cohort report with units, invariances, numerical checks and a bounded conclusion.
\end{itemize}
