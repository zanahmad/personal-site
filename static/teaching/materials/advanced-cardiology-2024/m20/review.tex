\section*{Field errors, conserved moments and correlation invariance --- review sheet}
Use this after attempting the worked examples and practice. Explain each claim in words before calculating.

\section*{Key ideas}
\begin{itemize}
\item Present one bounded question with traceable evidence.
\item Correlation, field error and conservation measure different properties.
\item Undefined metrics need explicit reporting.
\item Initial-condition consistency belongs in a conservation audit.
\item A smaller supported claim is preferable to an unsupported broad one.
\end{itemize}
\section*{Common errors and repairs}
\textbf{Treating correlation one as zero numerical error.} Compute amplitude/offset-sensitive metrics and inspect the field.

\textbf{Replacing an undefined correlation with zero.} Report why the denominator vanishes.

\textbf{Calling synthetic-reference agreement biological validation.} State the missing measurement and model evidence.

\section*{Retrieve without your notes}
\begin{itemize}
\item What does a mean-only field preserve?
\item What makes a limitation useful?
\end{itemize}
\section*{Readiness check}
\begin{itemize}
\item Present a bounded research claim with its model identity, evidence and limitations.
\item Calculate and contrast field error, mean balance and correlation on transparent counterexamples.
\item Review a peer's evidence chain and propose a concrete next test.
\item Distinguish a demonstrated result from a hypothesis or optional research extension.
\end{itemize}
