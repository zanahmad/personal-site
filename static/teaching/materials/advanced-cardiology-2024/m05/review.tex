\section*{Finite-volume advection and diffusion --- review sheet}
Use this after attempting the worked examples and practice. Explain each claim in words before calculating.

\section*{Key ideas}
\begin{itemize}
\item Store cell averages and update amounts through shared interface fluxes.
\item Advection follows a prescribed velocity; diffusion follows a negative concentration gradient.
\item Boundary flux determines the correct total-amount account.
\item Conservation, positivity, direction and accuracy require separate checks.
\end{itemize}
\section*{Common errors and repairs}
\textbf{Treating all pulse smoothing as physical diffusion.} Upwind discretization can smooth even with D=\allowbreak{}0; examine refinement.

\textbf{Keeping the same time step after halving cell width.} Recompute both the advective and diffusive restrictions.

\section*{Retrieve without your notes}
\begin{itemize}
\item What survives when all cell balances are summed?
\item Why can a stable conservative calculation still need refinement?
\end{itemize}
\section*{Readiness check}
\begin{itemize}
\item Derive a cell balance with a single shared flux at each interface.
\item Distinguish transport by bulk velocity from diffusion down a concentration gradient.
\item Check a boundary budget, constant state and nonnegative solution separately.
\item Implement a conservative update and predict the effects of changing velocity, diffusion or boundary conditions.
\end{itemize}
