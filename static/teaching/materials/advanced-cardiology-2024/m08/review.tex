\section*{Streamfunctions on a mapped chamber domain --- review sheet}
Use this after attempting the worked examples and practice. Explain each claim in words before calculating.

\section*{Key ideas}
\begin{itemize}
\item Prescribed kinematics, solved dynamics and physiological validation are distinct.
\item Shared-face conservation and sampled-velocity divergence test different operations.
\item A matching port flow does not determine interior motion.
\item Mesh sensitivity and boundary-input sensitivity answer different questions.
\item A proposed report should state exactly which equations, observables and references were tested.
\end{itemize}
\section*{Common errors and repairs}
\textbf{Calling any anatomically suggestive flow plot an atrial CFD solution.} State whether momentum was actually solved and whether geometry and boundary data are measured.

\textbf{Interpreting V/Q as every particle's residence time.} It is a bulk ratio; trajectories and exit-time distributions require additional analysis.

\textbf{Equating zero net flux with zero local divergence.} Local positive and negative errors can cancel; inspect a local norm as well.

\section*{Retrieve without your notes}
\begin{itemize}
\item What does the current diagnostic say about momentum?
\item Can two fields share a bulk turnover scale and differ internally?
\end{itemize}
\section*{Readiness check}
\begin{itemize}
\item Document geometry, port flow and model omissions for a synthetic chamber.
\item Check shared-face flux conservation and velocity-derived divergence as distinct diagnostics.
\item Measure derivative reconstruction error and compare a boundary-input sensitivity.
\item Explain why neither conservation nor grid refinement guarantees momentum balance or physiological validity.
\item Prepare a proposed effort-based CFD verification and interpretation report with a defensible claim scope.
\end{itemize}
