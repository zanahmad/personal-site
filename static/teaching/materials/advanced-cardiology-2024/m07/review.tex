\section*{Planar viscous flow and boundary-value discretization --- review sheet}
Use this after attempting the worked examples and practice. Explain each claim in words before calculating.

\section*{Key ideas}
\begin{itemize}
\item Incompressibility is a local volume constraint, not a complete velocity law.
\item The reduced momentum equation plus wall values selects the profile.
\item Flow per width and total flow have different units.
\item A nodally exact quadratic can still have a quadrature error.
\end{itemize}
\section*{Common errors and repairs}
\textbf{Using a pressure drop where the equation requires a pressure gradient.} Divide pressure drop by channel length to obtain G.

\textbf{Reporting an order-two profile error from the trapezoidal flux plot.} The plotted error belongs to integration; the quadratic nodal solution is exact to roundoff in this scheme.

\section*{Retrieve without your notes}
\begin{itemize}
\item What changes when viscosity doubles at fixed gradient and walls?
\item Which test checks nonzero boundary insertion?
\end{itemize}
\section*{Readiness check}
\begin{itemize}
\item Explain divergence-free velocity as the local constant-density volume balance.
\item Derive planar Poiseuille flow from a stated reduced momentum balance and two wall conditions.
\item Check SI units, no-slip walls, flow rate and limiting cases in a finite-difference boundary-value solve.
\item Distinguish algebraic residual, nodal profile error and flow-integration error.
\end{itemize}
