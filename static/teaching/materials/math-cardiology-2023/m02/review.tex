Use this after attempting the worked examples and practice. Explain each claim in words before calculating.

\section*{Key ideas}
\begin{itemize}
\item Flow responds to an along-path pressure drop and resistance.
\item Compliance links stored-volume change to distending-pressure change.
\item Inflow minus outflow accounts for storage.
\item The simplest useful spatial model depends on the question.
\end{itemize}
\section*{Common errors and repairs}
\textbf{Compliance and resistance are interchangeable.} Their units differ: mL/mmHg versus mmHg s/mL; one represents storage and the other a flow relation.

\textbf{Equal flows imply zero volume.} They imply unchanged volume.
