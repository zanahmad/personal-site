\section*{Calibrating a flow law and constructing a classifier --- review sheet}
Use this after attempting the worked examples and practice. Explain each claim in words before calculating.

\section*{Key ideas}
\begin{itemize}
\item Calibration estimates a model parameter; classification predicts a label.
\item Parameter and error units matter.
\item Freeze training choices before held-out evaluation.
\item Directional error counts add information beyond overall accuracy.
\end{itemize}
\section*{Common errors and repairs}
\textbf{A small training error validates the mechanism.} It only shows fit to those observations; test new predictions and missing mechanisms.

\textbf{Compute normalization from all rows.} Estimate it on training rows and reuse the same values on held-out rows.

\section*{Retrieve without your notes}
\begin{itemize}
\item What does G=\allowbreak{}.05 L/(min mmHg) predict at a 20 mmHg difference?
\item Can all-zero pressure differences identify G?
\item What does a confusion-table row count?
\end{itemize}
\section*{Readiness check}
\begin{itemize}
\item Fit and interpret one conductance parameter with units.
\item Distinguish a numerical flow prediction from a categorical prediction.
\item Separate training examples from held-out cases before fitting.
\item Calculate and interpret an error summary and a confusion count.
\end{itemize}
