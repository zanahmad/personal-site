Chapters 6–9

Notes, practice and available slides

Midterm 2 preparation

Midterm 2 is November 12 and covers Chapters 6–9. Each chapter below has detailed notes, a shorter exam-prep guide, lecture slides and matched section links. Chapter 6 now has full detailed notes in addition to its original concise review and group problems.

  1. Understand the framework and conditions. Use detailed notes and matching slides to explain the target parameter, sampling design, source of variability and assumptions behind each method.
  2. Recall the main ideas. Use the quick guide for a short review. Cover an equation and try to reconstruct it and state when it applies; follow its page links when a step is unclear.
  3. Attempt existing practice independently. Keep solutions closed, or stop before worked pages. Choose the method before calculating, and include a conclusion in context.
  4. Check released solutions and revisit gaps. Compare the reasoning as well as the answer. Return to the linked explanation, then retry the part you missed without the solution.
  5. Mix methods. Revisit questions from different chapters and explain why each asks for a sampling probability, confidence interval or hypothesis test, and whether observations are independent or paired.

Chapter 9 notes are available for preparation; the Chapter 9 lecture is Wednesday, November 4. The practice and solutions listed below are the materials released so far.
